## Access and accounting

`cycle-access.ts` requires a configured `ACCESS_CYCLE` (e.g., Fall 2027), existing non-demo household, signed-in administrator matching `DEMO_OWNER_EMAIL` and an owner auth link. Direct $0 grants require reason and idempotency key; complimentary/Founding Family invitations target a specific household, are hash-only, single-use and seven-day expiry, and require that household's signed-in owner to claim. A serialized/advisory-locked cap reserves no more than 25 distinct **lifetime combined** households, including invitations, even after revocation. No shareable/public grant link or client-supplied terms. Active access ends before August 1 following the named fall term and can be revoked; revocation does not erase accounting. Household deletion cascades access/invitations/assistant usage while de-identifying the household foreign key on financial orders and audit records; the cash ledger remains for reconciliation/retention review.

Orders, consideration/payment/refund/provider-fee/net-cash entries, and audit events are append-only/idempotent. One pending order per household-cycle is reused through a provider idempotency key; stale pending sessions need a reviewed expiration path. Stripe's pending order, verified payment and refunds reconcile in a signed, five-minute-tolerance raw-body webhook. Payment requires the configured server-only price identifier, expected cents, USD, paid status, matching household/cycle/order/session metadata, and a verified signature; redirect alone does nothing. Full refunds revoke paid entitlements. Fees are *pending* until a separately verified provider report is recorded by an owner through `reconcileProviderFee`; initial net cash is provisional and explicitly fee-pending. `reconciliationStatus` derives pending/reconciled/exception from entries rather than claiming a fee was known. Exceptions are retained in webhook events and summarized in owner UI. Final production work: review Stripe event semantics, retries, async and dispute/chargeback handling, provider fee import, checkout session expiration, entitlements enforcement, and reporting.

Hosted Stripe Checkout may show Apple Pay/Google Pay when Stripe account, device, country and verified domain support them. There is **no direct wallet code**. Sign-in (Google/Apple/Microsoft or email magic link) is separate from payment and inbox access; provider buttons remain gated by `AUTH_PROVIDERS` and Supabase settings/callback allowlisting. No credentials were configured.

## Connected mail (contract only)

`connected-mail.ts` defines Gmail/Microsoft metadata-only adapters which **throw by default**. There are no OAuth callback routes, provider credentials, or mailbox API calls. A future vetted callback must obtain explicit versioned owner consent and verified account identity; it must never pass browser-asserted tokens to `storeConnectedMail`. Tokens are AES-256-GCM encrypted before persistence, key versioned; raw account identifiers and message IDs are HMAC digests. Encryption key must be a secret manager key; rotation/re-encryption and provider-token revocation remain unimplemented. An internal re-consent method accepts newly verified OAuth tokens for the same hashed account after explicit owner consent; no callback invokes it yet. Local revocation immediately erases the stored encrypted token and evidence, but is **not a remote OAuth grant revocation**. Owner deletion removes all local connection/evidence rows; minimal control events remain until household deletion. Evidence purge is 30 days and needs a reviewed scheduler.

Allowlist is an intersection of curated active exact sender policy and `verified_mail_senders` linked to a recently verified official institutional source (including reviewed vendor record), with exact domain match. A future curation workflow must verify records; no arbitrary sender-domain query is permitted. Adapter scopes include at most 50 metadata envelopes per incremental checkpoint. Providers that cannot restrict server-side must retrieve minimal headers only, discard nonmatching metadata immediately, and never retrieve unrelated bodies. Only digest, institutional reference, domain, observation time and approved/quarantined status are retained; no subject, body, attachment or recipient is stored. Mail observations are **not** independent research certification or automatic task completion. A future connection UI should show consent, scopes, allowlist, re-consent/failure, local revoke/delete and retention controls before enabling sync.

**Assisted forwarding-rule fallback specification:** In the household's mail provider UI, the owner (not software installed on a desktop) elects to create a rule matching only explicitly displayed, approved school/vendor sender addresses; destination is a separately issued household alias after owner consent. Show a dry-run preview and verify sender/authentication on server; never set catch-all forwarding or search arbitrary mail. Provide instructions for editing/deleting the provider-side rule, revoking/rotating the alias, and understanding that a local revoke does not delete a provider-side rule. This is a design specification, not an installer or deployed ingress.

## Ask Campus Passage

`/ask` and `/api/ask` are household-authenticated review UI/API. The deterministic fallback matches question terms against a student's tracked-school action titles, and quotes only current, applicable, certified research with a current official source, term and verification freshness. Unknown, sensitive or suspected prompt-injection inputs refuse. No action submission, eligibility/deadline calculation, payment or record changes. The interface for a future provider-neutral model exists, but any model output must equal the deterministic sourced answer; no external model key or call is required. Daily usage is capped at 20 questions/household and 100 cents placeholder model budget; only count/status/cost metadata is logged, never prompt, response, chain-of-thought, mailbox contents or student attributes. Connected-mail evidence is deliberately not fed into answers yet; it cannot independently certify source facts. Expand only after privacy and citation/claim validation review.
